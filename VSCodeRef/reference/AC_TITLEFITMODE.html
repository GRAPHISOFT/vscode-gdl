ac_titlefitmodeinteger

Used by Drawing Title.

When AC_ManualPosition = 0, Archicad sets title position and size (AC_LayoutRect, AC_GridRect, AC_DrawingRect) by method set in this parameter. Manual movement of the drawing from the fit position is signalled by Archicad setting AC_ManualPosition = 1.

  • 1 - fit to drawing, the Drawing Title will be placed at the Drawing bounding box´s lower left corner with the desired rotation.

    Moving or rotating drawing: the Drawing Title object will be transformed too.

  • 2 - fit to layout, the Drawing Title will be placed on the Layout´s lower left corner. Rotation angle = 0°.

    Moving or rotating drawing: the Drawing Title will remain in the same position.

  • 3 - fit to grid cell, the Drawing Title will be placed on the Cell´s lower left corner. Rotation angle = 0°.

    Moving or rotating drawing: the left bottom corner of the new bounding box has to be computed, and the Title has to be shifted with the difference vector of the old and the new corners.

  • 4 - custom, no fitting.
