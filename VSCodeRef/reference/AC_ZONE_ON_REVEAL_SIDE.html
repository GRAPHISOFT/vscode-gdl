ac_zone_on_reveal_sideboolean

Controls which of the zones on the two sides of the wall owns this opening. Used in schedule fields calculated by Archicad. See also gs_bOpeningToZone, the "WINDOW_DOOR_ZONE_RELEV_OF_OWNER" REQUEST.

1: Opening belongs to the zone on the reveal side.

0: Opening belongs to the zone on the opposite side.
